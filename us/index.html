Our story

From a street-corner pho stall

In 1986 our grandfather carried his broth pot on a shoulder pole to the corner of Lo Duc street before dawn. Regulars called it "phở gánh" — the stall pho — and the name stuck.

  1. 1986Grandfather sells pho from a shoulder-pole stall
  2. 1998The first shop opens with six wooden tables
  3. 2015The second generation keeps the same broth recipe
  4. TodayThe third generation runs the kitchen every morning

— The Phở Gánh family

The broth

A 12-hour pot of broth

Beef marrow bones simmer on a low flame from 5 p.m., skimmed constantly for a clear broth. Charred ginger and onion, cinnamon, star anise and black cardamom go in before dawn to keep their aroma.
